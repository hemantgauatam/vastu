Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Kuber Vastu account from the app. Follow the steps below to remove your account and its associated data.

Updated 5 November 2026

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Kuber Vastu and go to Profile.
  2. Log in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Scroll down and select Delete account.
  4. Enter your current password in the confirmation dialog.
  5. Select Delete account to confirm. Wait for the message confirming that your account has been deleted.
Deletion is permanent. Your account and its order history cannot be restored through the app. Logging out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

If the password is incorrect or the service is unavailable, the app shows an error. Your account is not treated as deleted until the server confirms deletion.

2. Data that is deleted

When deletion succeeds, the app removes the following records linked to your account from its account database:

  • Your profile and login details, including your name, email address, phone number, place, gender, user type, username and stored password hash.
  • Your active login sessions.
  • Your order and purchase history stored in the app database.
  • Reviews linked to your account and associated reports, including reports you submitted.

The app signs you out and clears the locally saved account profile, session, order history and cart on that device. You will no longer be able to log in using the deleted account.

3. Other data and services

Older reviews submitted without an account link cannot be automatically identified as yours. If you want help removing one, include enough detail to identify the review when contacting support.

Account deletion does not erase records held independently by payment providers, such as Razorpay, or copies of emails already sent. Those providers manage their records under their own policies. Deleting an account does not automatically request a refund or cancel an order.

Floor-plan images, exported files, captures and other files saved separately on your device may remain after account deletion. Remove any unwanted files using the app's available deletion controls or your device's file and photo tools.

4. Need help with deletion?

If you cannot access your account or complete these steps, contact Kuber Vastu using the contact details on our official website. State that you are requesting account deletion and provide your registered email address or username so support can identify the account. Account ownership may need to be verified.
