1. About this policy

Kuber Vastu offers Vastu guidance, floor-plan direction mapping, a live camera view, educational content, and information about Vastu remedies. This policy describes information associated with these features.

2. Information you provide

When registering for an account, you may provide your name, gender, email address, phone number, place, user type, username, and password. Account details are sent to the service to process registration and sign-in.

You may also select a floor-plan image or document for use with the app’s mapping tools. Information you submit through an external website or service is subject to that service’s policies.

3. App permissions

Some features need access to device capabilities. Your device may ask you to grant the following permissions:

  • Camera: to display the live Vastu overlay.
  • Photos and files: to select a floor-plan image or document.
  • Location: to support compass heading functionality where required by the device.
  • Motion sensors: to keep the compass and direction display aligned.

A permission request does not by itself mean the associated data is uploaded. Permissions can be reviewed in your device settings.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, including account registration and sign-in, floor-plan analysis, direction displays, reports, and access to app content.

The app communicates with its backend over HTTPS. Network communication is also used to load content such as images, fonts, and videos.

5. Third-party services

The app includes YouTube content and links to external services, including social media, WhatsApp, and the Kuber Vastu website. When you use these features, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

Please review the privacy policies of any external service before providing personal information to it.

6. Your choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

For questions about account information, correction, retention, or deletion, contact Kuber Vastu through the official website. Do not include your password when contacting support.

7. Policy updates

This page may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its information practices. Review the policy when using new features or granting additional permissions.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests concerning Kuber Vastu, please use the contact information provided on our official website.
